Who we are

MORE THAN A
NAME ON A JERSEY.

IGNITE AWOKEN CLUB is an esports development and sponsorship initiative focused on helping promising teams turn potential into consistent performance.

01 / DEVELOPMENT

Build the foundation

Guidance, structure, accountability and a clear path for teams that are ready to improve.

02 / OPPORTUNITY

Back the underdogs

We look beyond popularity and focus on commitment, potential, teamwork and competitive ambition.

03 / PARTNERSHIP

Grow together

Transparent expectations and responsible resource planning help create sustainable partnerships.

What we offer

SUPPORT WITH
A PURPOSE.

Every partnership should have clear goals, measurable progress and shared responsibility.

Team sponsorship

Support for selected teams based on eligibility, performance potential and an agreed development plan.

  • Structured sponsorship review
  • Team identity and club collaboration
  • Progress and financial monitoring
  • Performance and conduct expectations

Player development

We want to discover players who are serious about improving and contributing to a positive competitive environment.

  • Player and roster visibility
  • Competitive opportunities
  • Professional conduct standards
  • Long-term growth mindset
